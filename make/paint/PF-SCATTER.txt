PF scatter — board-frame kit (desk)
===================================

Paint the geometric ruins to sit around the future nave. Match the cinematic plates:
charcoal stone, soot, yellow-rust on crates and barrels, selective auramite on the gate mark.

Walls / tile / pillar / crater: charcoal + dust. Not bright grey, not static grass.
Barricade / crate / barrel: rust and oil, not factory orange.
Walkway: iron + rust, thin gold only on a rail if you want the Forge flash.
Markers / placard: same locked gate as PF-PLATE — gold on charcoal.

Photograph a test table, not a catalog shot, until pack #1 exists.
No aquila. No chapter badges. No named shrine titles on the pieces.
