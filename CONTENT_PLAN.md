## Rules of focus (honest)

- **One video = one main thing** (box/convert, paint technique, hero unit, or army update).  
- BA + Knights of the Chalice in the **same** video = OK (one crusade).  
- Orks, Gundam, Star Wars, AoS, Kill Team = fine for the **channel**, not mixed into a BA episode.  
- Shorts pull tips from every long video (airbrush, jump convert, before/after).
