# Environment map — research notes (2026-09-05)

Public facts only. **Do not copy** Games Workshop Event Companion maps, GHB battleplan diagrams, or Approved Ops killzone maps onto the site.

The website planner uses **original Forge starting arrangements** plus **published table sizes** and **widely reported footprint dimensions**. Game names are compatibility labels.

## Warhammer 40,000 — 11th edition (launched 20 June 2026)

- Table for 2,000 pts: **60 × 44 in** (unchanged).
- Competitive tables use **area-terrain footprints** (templates), not “whatever ruin you own.”
- Widely reported kit for a standard table (**16 pieces**):
  - 4 × large rectangle **7 × 11.5 in**
  - 2 × large right triangle **8 × 11.5 in**
  - 4 × medium rectangle **6 × 4 in**
  - 2 × long line **10 × 2.5 in**
  - 4 × short line **6 × 2 in**
- Launch talk: about **five primary mission types × three layouts** (15 configs) plus a larger Event Companion set. Those **published maps stay off this site**.
- Deployment in the planner: generic **long edges / short edges / corners** only.

Sources (news / third-party guides, not rulebook scans): [Spikey Bits](https://spikeybits.com/11th-edition-40k-terrain-rules-rumors-guide/), [Bell of Lost Souls](https://www.belloflostsouls.net/2026/04/warhammer-40k-11th-edition-terrain-rules-revealed-redefining-terrain-on-the-tabletop.html), [Voidborn Prints](https://voidbornprints.com/blogs/guides-tutorials/building-the-new-battlefield-terrain-rules-table-construction-in-warhammer-40-000-11th-edition).

## Age of Sigmar — 4th edition · GHB 2025–26

- Battle (2,000 pts): **60 × 44 in**, about **8 terrain features**.
- Vanguard / 1,000 pts often **44 × 30 in**, about **4 features**.
- Siege / 3,000 pts often **72 × 48 in**.
- Handbook has named battleplans (Passing Seasons, Roiling Roots, …). **We do not use those names or diagrams.**
- Planner starts: long-edge camps, short-edge, corners, open mid — original 8-piece woods/ruins.

## Horus Heresy — Age of Darkness 3rd (rulebook July 2025)

- Open battle: classic **72 × 48 in (6×4)**.
- Core pack: **three missions** and **three deployment map types** (long-edge line, short-edge corridor, diagonal/vanguard). We use those **types**, not the printed maps.
- Zone Mortalis (Journal Tactica 2026): **~48 × 48**, **12 in sectors**, walls/doors by density — no fixed official city map. Planner: 4×4 grid + movable wall segments.

## Kill Team (current Approved Ops year)

- Board: **30 × 22 in**.
- Official killzones (Volkus, Gallowdark, Tomb World, Bheta-Decima) have **six layouts each**. **Not reproduced.**
- Planner: original open yards / split halls / streets / rooms. Heavy vs light shapes you can drag.

## Table surfaces (planner themes)

Visual table mats for setup photos and PNG export. Not official GW boards — Forge colour ideas only.

| Id | Look | Idea |
|----|------|------|
| `grass` | Green meadow / fields | BA vs Orks open ground; darker hedges under ruins |
